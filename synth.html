The Planetary Synthesiser

A modular synthesiser whose oscillators are the planets, live from the sky of this moment. Choose which quantity sets each note, which way it runs, how the notes are tuned, and wire the sky into the sound with the modulation matrix.

Modulation matrix πλέγμα

Each row is a live quantity of the sky, each column a parameter of every planet’s voice. Tap a cell to route one to the other with an amount; gold is positive, green reversed. The dots show where the planets sit on each source right now.

 
How it works

The planets’ positions are computed from NASA JPL orbital elements for the date shown; moons circle at their true periods. Everything is synthesised live in your browser. Headphones or a 5.1 system recommended.
