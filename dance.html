The progression ἁρμονία

Home, then the chords that Zeus, Kronos and Poseidon stand on in the zodiac, seen from Earth.

The band θίασος

Pitches use Pythagorean tuning in the style's Greek mode: Hypodorian (minor) for trance and tango, Phrygian (the modern Dorian) for house and cha-cha, Dorian (the modern Phrygian) for techno and fandango, Lydian (the modern major) for the waltz, and Hypophrygian (the modern Mixolydian) for samba, swing and jive. The waltz and fandango are in three; swing and jive swing their eighths in triplets. The fandango follows the Andalusian cadence, with the sky choosing its first two chords. The key follows the Sun's sign. Builds start when an aspect between planets tightens and drop as it comes exact. If the sky stays quiet for 32 bars, the band builds anyway. Headphones recommended.
